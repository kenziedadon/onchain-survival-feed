framing

honest framing

This feed shows a heuristics-based survival-horizon score for freshly created Aerodrome V2 liquidity pools, not a certainty, not a rug guarantee, and not a price prediction or trading advice.

The score is a rules-based estimate of how survivable a fresh pool's setup looks over the next stretch, inferred from checkable on-chain behavior around creation. It is calibrated by observed outcomes over time, not by anything mystical.

A higher score means the setup looks more survivable under the current heuristic. A lower score means the setup looks less survivable under the current heuristic. Many pools with high scores still fail, and many with low scores survive — the signal is weak and improving, not decisive.

Fail-closed behavior: when inputs are missing or RPC reads fail, the score returns a neutral sentinel — never a confident low or high. The feed never FOMO-pushes a pool because a node timed out.

Operator bypass: verified trusted deployer addresses short-circuit to a trusted score. This is an explicit, narrow trust path — not a blanket safe signal.

limitations

  • heuristics only — no labeled outcomes yet (rug/decay/survived labels pending).
  • funding-tx timestamp resolution is partial — some pools show ttl "N/A".
  • dependent on public RPC (mainnet.base.org) when real RPC reads are enabled.
  • deterministic fixture run until real RPC reads are wired.
